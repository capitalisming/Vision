Optional sensor module: check page

Reads the HC-SR04 on the Raspberry Pi over WebSocket. Open this page from the laptop's local server (node serve.mjs, then http://localhost:8000/hw.html): an https page cannot connect to the Pi.

1. Connect

Not connected.

2. Live reading

Distance (calibrated, 0.5 s median)
— mm
Raw sensor (0.5 s median)
— mm
Light (camera estimate, not a light meter)
— lux

3. Calibrate (once per sensor mounting)

Sit so that your eyes are 500 mm from the screen (measure with a ruler), press Save.

4. Experiment 5: sensor vs tape

The leader measures the tape distance from the outer eye corner to the screen. Press Record three times per distance; between presses the participant leans back and sits again. Left eye covered with an eye patch; hands off the desk in front of the sensor.

Camera column stays empty unless the laptop camera is started and calibrated for this participant in the app.

Error per tape distance

error % = (sensorMm − tapeMm) / tapeMm × 100. Criterion fixed before the first measurement: mean absolute error ≤ 10 % at every distance.
